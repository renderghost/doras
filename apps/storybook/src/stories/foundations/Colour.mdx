# Colour

Every semantic colour, in each theme. Each swatch is drawn with the real custom property inside that theme, on its own surface.

Text is at least 4.5:1 against its surfaces, or 7:1 in the high-contrast themes. Field edges, focus rings and meaningful icons are at least 3:1. The token tests check every pairing in all four themes.

Colour is never the only signal: errors, success and automated suggestions always have an icon or words too.
